How paper practice works

Write the Japanese on your paper. Romaji typing is optional. Press Done to see both Japanese and romaji, then compare with your paper. Use Listen or Slow to hear the Japanese. Mark your paper answer correct or needing practice, or simply move to the next word.

Typed romaji accepts long vowels such as otousan / otōsan and keeki / kēki. Blank romaji is not counted as wrong.
